## **PART I - FRONT MATTER AND GOVERNANCE**

### **1\. Introduction and Purpose**

This document, titled **"The CEO Influencers - Comprehensive Legal, Compliance, and Data Protection Framework"**, establishes the unified legal, regulatory, data protection, security, and governance standards governing the operations of The CEO Influencers LLC ("the Company").

This Framework is intended to serve as the **single authoritative reference** for:

- Board oversight and fiduciary governance
- Legal and contractual compliance
- Privacy and data protection obligations
- Information security standards
- Recording consent and data processing practices
- Content protection and intellectual property enforcement
- Breach notification and incident response readiness

This Framework consolidates and integrates the Company's core compliance domains into one cohesive governance instrument suitable for executive oversight, regulatory review, external audit, and enterprise risk management.

### **3\. Scope and Applicability**

This Framework applies to:

- All employees, officers, contractors, instructors, and administrators
- All clients and users, including U.S. and international clients
- All personal data and confidential information processed
- All session recordings, communications, and stored materials

Compliance with this Framework is mandatory and forms part of all contractual, employment, and service relationships.

### **4\. Regulatory and Legal Framework**

The Company is primarily governed by the laws of the United States, including:

- Federal Trade Commission Act (FTC Act)
- U.S. contract law principles
- U.S. consumer protection laws

As a Nevada-registered entity, the Company is further subject to:

- Nevada business record retention requirements (minimum seven years)
- Nevada data security and privacy obligations

Due to its global client base, the Company is also subject to:

- EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Article 3 (extraterritorial scope)
- UK GDPR under the Data Protection Act 2018

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the Company acts as the **Data Controller** and applies equivalent protections across all systems to ensure consistency and risk reduction.

### **5\. Definitions**

For purposes of this Framework:

- **Personal Data** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
- **Processing** means any operation performed on personal data, whether automated or manual.
- **Client** means any individual or entity accessing or purchasing Company services.
- **Recording** means any audio, video, or screen recording of sessions.
- **Data Controller** means The CEO Influencers LLC.
- **Data Processor** means any third-party processing data on behalf of the Company.
- **Confidential Information** includes all non-public business or client information.
- **Content** includes all proprietary materials, recordings, documents, and methodologies.
- **Breach** means any unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, or compromise of data.

#### **6.1 Board Oversight**

#### **6.2 Executive Management**

#### **6.3 Data Protection Lead**

- Privacy documentation

A formal Data Protection Officer is not required at this stage.

Responsible for infrastructure security, access control, encryption, monitoring, and incident response support.

#### **6.5 Instructors and Coaches**

Subject to confidentiality, recording access limitations, secure device requirements, and anti-piracy obligations.

The Company manages data through the following lifecycle stages:

- Collection - onboarding, payments, communications, recordings
- Storage - encrypted databases and secure VPS storage
- Access - role-based, logged, MFA-protected
- Retention - unified seven-year retention model

## **PART IV - RECORDING CONSENT, DATA PROCESSING, AND RETENTION**

### **29\. Recording Consent Framework**

#### **29.1 Notice of Recording**

The Company may record certain coaching, advisory, mentoring, or training sessions, including one-to-one and group sessions, for the purposes of service delivery, quality assurance, contractual performance, and internal knowledge management.

Clients are hereby notified in advance that all sessions are recorded.

#### **29.2 Consent Requirement**

Where required by applicable law, explicit consent is obtained prior to recording. By participating in recorded sessions, Clients acknowledge and agree to the recording, storage, and processing of such recordings in accordance with this Framework.

#### **29.3 Legal Basis**

Recordings are processed under the following lawful bases:

- Contractual necessity, where recordings are integral to service delivery
- Legitimate interests, including quality control, dispute resolution, and business recordkeeping
- Consent, where legally required

### **30\. Data Processing Roles and Responsibilities**

#### **30.1 Data Controller**

The CEO Influencers LLC acts as the Data Controller for all personal data processed in connection with recorded sessions.

#### **30.2 Data Processors**

Third-party service providers, including hosting and infrastructure providers, act as Data Processors under contractual obligations to process data solely on the Company's instructions and to implement appropriate safeguards.

#### **30.3 Internal Access Controls**

Access to recordings is restricted to:

- Authorized instructors
- Designated administrative personnel
- The applicable Client

Access is granted strictly on a need-to-know basis and is logged for audit purposes.

### **31\. Recording Storage, Access, and Downloads**

#### **31.1 Storage**

Recordings are stored on secure infrastructure hosted on Hostinger VPS environments, protected by encryption, access controls, and monitoring.

#### **31.2 Instructor Access**

Instructors may access and, where operationally necessary, download recordings solely for the purpose of delivering contracted services.

Downloaded recordings must be stored only on Company-approved, encrypted devices and must not be copied, shared, or transferred.

#### **31.3 Client Access**

Clients may be granted access to recordings, which may be watermarked and subject to access limitations.

Recordings are provided strictly for personal use and may not be redistributed.

#### **31.4 Monitoring**

Access to recordings is logged and monitored to detect unauthorized access, duplication, or distribution.

### **32\. Seven-Year Retention Model**

#### **32.1 Retention Period**

All recordings and associated personal data are retained for a minimum period of **seven (7) years** in accordance with U.S. business recordkeeping and legal requirements.

This unified retention model applies to:

- Session recordings
- Session notes and annotations
- Communications related to recorded sessions

#### **32.2 Justification**

Retention is justified based on:

- Contractual obligations
- Legal and regulatory requirements
- Dispute resolution
- Audit and compliance needs

#### **32.3 Secure Archiving**

During the retention period, recordings may be archived in secure storage with restricted access.

### **33\. Withdrawal of Consent and Legal Limitations**

#### **33.1 Withdrawal Requests**

Where consent is the lawful basis, Clients may request withdrawal of consent for future recordings.

#### **33.2 Limitations**

Withdrawal of consent does not apply retroactively where recordings must be retained to comply with legal obligations or to defend legal claims.

#### **33.3 Effect on Services**

Withdrawal of consent may limit the Company's ability to provide certain services that rely on recorded materials.

### **34\. Case Studies and Anonymization**

#### **34.1 Internal Knowledge Use**

The Company may use anonymized data derived from recordings for internal training, quality improvement, and knowledge management.

#### **34.2 Anonymization Standards**

All identifiers are removed prior to use, ensuring individuals cannot be reasonably identified.

#### **34.3 No Public Disclosure**

No recordings or identifiable data are published or shared externally without explicit, separate authorization.

## **PART V - BREACH NOTIFICATION AND INCIDENT RESPONSE**

### **35\. Definition of a Data Breach**

For purposes of this Framework, a **Data Breach** means any confirmed or reasonably suspected incident that results in:

- Unauthorized access to personal data or confidential information
- Unauthorized disclosure of personal data or confidential information
- Loss, destruction, or alteration of personal data
- Compromise of system security affecting data integrity or availability

This definition aligns with applicable U.S. data breach laws and **GDPR Article 4(12)**.

### **36\. Incident Detection and Classification**

#### **36.1 Detection**

Security incidents may be detected through:

- System monitoring and logs
- Alerts from hosting or infrastructure providers
- Reports from employees, instructors, or clients
- Third-party notifications

All suspected incidents must be reported immediately to executive management and the Data Protection Lead.

#### **36.2 Classification**

Incidents are classified based on:

- Type of data affected
- Number of individuals impacted
- Sensitivity of the data
- Likelihood of harm
- Legal and regulatory notification requirements

### **37\. Containment and Investigation**

#### **37.1 Immediate Containment**

Upon identification of a breach, the Company will:

- Isolate affected systems
- Disable compromised accounts or credentials
- Prevent further unauthorized access
- Preserve system logs and evidence

#### **37.2 Investigation**

A formal investigation may be conducted to determine:

- Root cause
- Scope and impact
- Systems and data affected
- Timeline of events
- Corrective actions required

All investigative actions are documented.

### **38\. Notification Obligations (United States and GDPR)**

#### **38.1 United States Notification**

Where required under applicable U.S. federal or state law, affected individuals and authorities will be notified without unreasonable delay.

Notification content will include:

- Description of the incident
- Types of data involved
- Steps taken to mitigate harm
- Recommended actions for affected individuals

#### **38.2 GDPR and UK GDPR Notification**

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, and the breach is likely to result in risk to individuals:

- The relevant supervisory authority will be notified within **72 hours** of awareness
- Affected individuals will be notified without undue delay where required

### **39\. Communication with Affected Clients**

All communications with affected clients will be:

- Clear and factual
- Free of speculation
- Consistent across channels
- Coordinated through executive management

The Company will provide guidance on steps clients can take to protect themselves where appropriate.

### **40\. Evidence Preservation and Post-Incident Review**

#### **40.1 Evidence Preservation**

All logs, records, and forensic data related to the incident will be preserved to support legal, regulatory, or insurance requirements.

#### **40.2 Post-Incident Review**

Following resolution, the Company will conduct a post-incident review to:

- Assess response effectiveness
- Identify control failures
- Implement corrective actions
- Update policies, training, or safeguards

Lessons learned will be documented and retained.
